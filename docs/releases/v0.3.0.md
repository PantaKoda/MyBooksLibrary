**MyBooksLibrary 0.3.0** runs on the new analysis engine, pdfbookmark 0.4.0, and explains in plain words why a field or a book's contents could not be read.

## Download

**`MyBooksLibrary-0.3.0-win64.zip`**, for Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit).
- **Install or upgrade:** unpack it anywhere and run `appMyBooksLibrary.exe`. To upgrade from 0.2.0, replace only the folder you unpacked the app into. Your library (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\MyBooksLibrary\MyBooksLibrary\Library`) stays where it is.
- **SmartScreen:** Windows may warn the first time, because the app is not code-signed yet.
- **`.sha256`:** the file next to the zip lets you check the download.

**New to the app?** Read `USER_GUIDE.md` in the zip (also [online](https://github.com/PantaKoda/MyBooksLibrary/blob/main/docs/USER_GUIDE.md)).

## New

- **New analysis engine: pdfbookmark 0.4.0** (from 0.3.0) (#40).
  - Books are read as before; the engine's changes are in what it reports (ISBNs, which the app does not show yet) and in how it is built.
  - **Books already in your library keep their results.** Importing the same PDF again does not read it again: select the book and use **More → Read title and authors again** and **More → Analyze contents again**, or import into a new library.
  - The OCR models' licence now comes with the engine (in `licenses\pdfbookmark\`).

## Fixed

- **No more "Why?" buttons** (#39).
  - A field the analysis could not fill now says why, under it, for example "No edition statement on the cover, title or copyright pages searched". This stays when you correct or clear the field.
  - The buttons say what they show: **Show candidates (*n*)** or **Show evidence**. There is no button when there is nothing more to show.
  - In a narrow inspector, the buttons move under the "where this came from" line instead of squeezing it.
  - On the **Contents** tab, **Why? (*n*)** is now **Analysis notes (*n*)**. When contents pages were found but the analysis could not settle on a table of contents, the summary says so instead of "No contents entries."
- **Windows "N" and "KN" editions should no longer need the Media Feature Pack** (#40). The engine's text-recognition library no longer uses Windows Media Foundation. Not yet tried on an N edition.

## Known limits

- **Search** covers titles, authors and contents entries, not the full text of the books.
- **OCR** of scanned books is slow (several seconds per page) and uses up to about 2.5 GB of memory.
- **Some scanned books are read poorly:** titles with several candidates, or contents found but not settled. The app says so rather than guess, and every field and entry can be corrected.
- **ISBNs** are read by the engine but not stored or shown yet.
- **Windows only** for now: macOS and Linux need native builds of the OCR library.
- **Third-party licences** are in `NOTICE.txt` and `licenses\` in the zip.
